I am a PhD student in the Department of Mechanical Engineering at The University of Texas at Austin, affiliated with [Texas Robotics](https://robotics.utexas.edu/). I work in the [Advanced Robotics Technologies for Surgery (ARTS) Lab](https://sites.utexas.edu/arts-lab/) under the supervision of Dr. Farshid Alambeigi. My research explores the co-design of flexible implants and surgical robotic systems, leveraging concentric tube robots and optical fiber sensing for orthopedic applications.

I received both my B.S. and M.S. in Mechanical Engineering from UT Austin, where I was an active member of the Formula SAE Electric team. I also gained industry experience through engineering internships at Tesla, Dell Technologies, SpaceX, and Amazon, working on high-performance mechanical systems across energy, aerospace, and consumer hardware.

My doctoral work aims to improve orthopedic fixation procedures by designing and developing flexible implants and robotic drilling tools that enable fixation in previously inaccessible anatomical regions. I am passionate about translating rigorous engineering fundamentals into real-world clinical impact through hands-on hardware development and experimentation.

I am always open to collaborations and conversations — feel free to reach out!
