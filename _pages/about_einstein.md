I am a PhD student in the Department of Mechanical Engineering at The University of Texas at Austin, affiliated with [Texas Robotics](https://robotics.utexas.edu/). I work in the [Advanced Robotics Technologies for Surgery (ARTS) Lab](https://www.farshidalambeigi.com/) under the supervision of Dr. Farshid Alambeigi. My research sits at the intersection of mechanical design, surgical robotics, and sensing — with a focus on flexible implants, concentric tube robots, optical sensors, and orthopedic surgical robotics.

I completed both my B.S. and M.S. in Mechanical Engineering at UT Austin. During my undergraduate years, I was an active member of the UT Austin FSAE Electric team and gained industry experience through engineering internships at Tesla, Dell Technologies, SpaceX, and Amazon.

My doctoral work aims to improve orthopedic fixation procedures by designing and developing flexible implants and robotic drilling tools that enable fixation in previously inaccessible anatomical regions. I am passionate about translating rigorous engineering fundamentals into real-world clinical impact through hands-on hardware development and experimentation.

I am always open to collaborations and conversations — feel free to reach out!
